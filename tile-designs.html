Fresh looks for the bottle & cocktail tiles

The current tiles work, but they’re starting to feel a little flat — a small photo beside a block of text, the same shape for everything. Below are three directions each for bottles and cocktails, all built in our real brand (the cream, the Fraunces headlines, the terracotta accent), so they’d drop straight into the site. Same ingredients, different plating.

About the images: the warm amber frames are stand-ins showing where real bottle and cocktail photos go. A couple of tiles also show our current “no photo yet” striped placeholder, so you can see how each layout holds up before every item has a picture. Hover any tile to feel it.

A few look & feel notes

beyond the tiles
Photography

Photos are the upgrade

Every direction here gets dramatically better with real, consistent bottle and cocktail photos — same angle, same warm light. It’s the single highest-impact change we can make.

Consistency

Let bottles & cocktails rhyme

Right now they’re built a bit differently. Picking one card language for both — e.g. Portrait Shelf + Recipe Card — makes the whole site feel designed rather than assembled.

Flexibility

Offer a list view

The compact “Menu Line” could be a toggle beside the cards, so browsers can flip between rich cards and a fast scannable list — a small feature people love.

Hierarchy

One action per tile

The current cocktail tile has three buttons competing. Leading with a single clear action (and moving the rest to the detail page) makes tiles calmer and clicks more confident.

Motion

A gentle lift on hover

The subtle raise-and-shadow you feel hovering these tiles adds a premium, tactile quality with almost no performance cost. Worth keeping whichever direction we choose.

Warmth

Lean into the amber

The whiskey’s own color is our best untapped accent. Used lightly — a warm frame, a hover tint — it makes the site feel unmistakably about whiskey.
